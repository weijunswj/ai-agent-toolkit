# Continuous core discovery baseline

Explicit continuous admission preserves one builder responsible for framing, design, contract, construction and validation. Optional discovery is bounded and subordinate to that decision. Seven positive bounded-task qualifications are required for the cheaper OpenAI builder; UNKNOWN or NEGATIVE retains Sol 6.1 High.

All four registry-v3 profiles use explicit role/provider/model/reasoning tuples. Read the canonical registry and full accepted decision through the compiler/receipt CURRENT consumer. Inspect/compile is plan-only. Source tests, a matching model name or a JSON qualification flag do not establish a native tuple.

Design challenge follows material product/trust risk. Inherited stronger reviews remain due, especially the integrated strong leaf review of the discovery baseline. A fresh independent review of the exact candidate and the separate programme final audit remain required. Source construction does not launch a checker, that independent review or a helper and does not mint a candidate allowance.

Historical debt, unknown additional history, consumed attempts, custody and unresolved effects remain bound to the stable root. A manual first-admission overlay is not a generic history certificate. No restart or renamed task restores eligibility. Native proof, installed bootstrap invocation, fresh-consumer recovery and retirement/rollback are later explicit boundaries.
