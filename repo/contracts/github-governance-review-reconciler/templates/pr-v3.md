Tracker format contract: review evidence is inventory-first and disposition is
truthful, controller-owned, and exact-head bound.

Human-v2 PR presentation companion (normal current path): render Summary,
Programme position, What changed, Why, Scope, Out of scope, Validation,
Candidate / lineage, and Final status / what happens next. Repair, before/after,
budget, hosted, and recovery sections require explicit applicability flags.
The carrier embeds neither the descriptor nor the presentation model as
authority. The renderer supports PRE_NUMBER and controller-authorised BOUND
representations; provider assignment alone cannot create the BOUND state.
