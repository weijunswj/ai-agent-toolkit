# Toolkit Controller: roles and routes
Part of the [Toolkit Controller](../CONTROLLER.md); it binds like that file.
## Roles and routes
- Resolve DISCOVERY, DISCOVERY_HELPER, BUILDER, BUILDER_HELPER, required reviewers and DESIGN_CHALLENGE from the canonical [stack registry](../contracts/controller-kernel/stack-registry-v3.json): exact captured registry revision and pinned CURRENT selection. Model, effort and reviewer choices come only from it; never infer helpers from leads or use defaults/fallbacks.
- Launch every gate with model and effort pinned explicitly in its launch command/config, fresh in the controller's harness (Projects thread/Remote Control, or a same-tool Claude Code/Codex session). Exception: the gatekeeper may start the other harness's gate through that tool's CLI with model/effort pinned; route proof comes from that CLI's own launch record. Read the host's launch record (init event/session metadata) once before consequential work, never self-report or a headless run; inherited, missing or mismatched model/effort refuses start. With no host record, record "not checkable on this host". Verify actual route/effective config before effects. No unavailable route authorises a model/effort/provider fallback. Historical qualification overrides keep exact scope/immutable inputs. Appointment authorises session creation/archive. [Codex hold](../docs/HOST-CONTRACT.md).
- One gatekeeper per lane is a fresh session in the controller's harness on the mid tier at medium effort (`agent-cost-control`). It starts gate runs, waits in one blocking call (no polling or progress posts), and relays the report verbatim. It never judges findings, merges, closes, takes controller authority or a gate's verdict. Every report includes a Route line naming actual model and effort. This rule supplies no launcher or hook enforcement.
- Subagents are helpers, never gates. Pin model/effort in their files; verify run logs, never self-report. Helpers never launch agents (global rules); spawn depth stays 1 (`CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_SUBAGENT_SPAWN_DEPTH=1`; Codex `agents.max_depth = 1`, accepted by Codex 0.164; `multi_agent_v2` has none: policy plus hook). Nesting needs depth 2+ and an owner change. Coordinators/non-gate threads relay, never take a gate's verdict.
- **Merge-train lanes:** running a train (`toolkit-merge-train.cjs` build, catchup, verify, body, land) is mechanical: no G0 or G1, the small tier at medium effort (`agent-cost-control`). The lane never resolves a conflict or edits code; it ejects the member, whose builder fixes it. The train needs no G2 of its own once `verify` passes and CI is green; only files `verify` lists under `overlap` (two or more members change them) get a short delta review. A train holds at most 6 members (`build` refuses a seventh); later ready PRs board the next. A member that fails CI or its delta review is ejected at once and the rest carry on, rebuilt without it on a new train branch, never force-pushed. Never edit a train or member PR body while its CI runs; queue the edit until CI ends. A stacked train's PR targets the running train's branch (main's version check would call its fragments unfolded) until that train lands, then main, before `catchup`. The train's merge ask goes up where `land` and the merge run, never relayed (Projects: the train thread, `reference/cloud-hook-parity.md`). At boarding, copy each member's `Pack changes` lines into its manifest entry (`pack_changes`) so the train body carries them.
- Service tier is the controller's: future launches use Standard; active sessions stay unchanged.
- G2 begins after builder readiness; owner UAT follows the merge (gate rules). Resolve the full reviewer set from the same selection; each independently reviews the identical complete candidate, scope and evidence (correctness, execution, authority, privacy, recovery) in a fresh context. Never split scope/execution assignments, enforce a fixed count, omit members or copy another review as independent evidence. G2 has no helpers. DESIGN_CHALLENGE is only for unresolved consequential design risk, never a reviewer replacement or routine extra role.
- The owner may address G0/G1 directly or by faithful relay on the same assignment. Record each decision once with provenance; reject stale/competing input for that question.
