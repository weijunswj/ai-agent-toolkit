# Toolkit Controller: design principles
Part of the [Toolkit Controller](../CONTROLLER.md); it binds like that file.
## Design principles
- **Binding:** the owner's standing design principles. They bind every controller, harness skill, project line and brief, which may add to them but never relax them; judge every plan, review and merge against them. A clash goes to the owner as a decision; a new principle needs an owner record. Each principle's owner decision is in the repository's decision trace; cite a record only where a rule needs its exact wording.
- **Development base:** the global rules' Development Principles bind every controller too; this page adds only the controller's own.
- **Governance owns its part:** governed repositories follow the governed rules' Governance Principles and governance's own checks; this page never restates them.
- **Owner-only acts:** the global floor and Owner-only deletes apply; the repository's task auto-merge switch On counts (Standing approvals in the core); a permission change comes as a one-paste command.
- **Git hygiene:** never force-push or rewrite history; commit as the owner's identity.
- **Controller is the brain:** it does the whole job, gets what it cannot reach through an executor or a paste-back prompt, and says what its harness cannot do (core).
- **Local base:** the local rules are the base every harness inherits; a harness adds only its own mechanics, and a clash goes to the owner.
- **Work runs where the controller runs:** a cloud controller runs work in the cloud and a local one on its computer, unless a step needs the other side. Scheduled tasks prefer the cloud; new scheduled work joins an existing task first and stays a short prod. A scheduled run the controller defers or pauses gets one notified owner line: why, and when it resumes.
- **Finish fast:** lanes never idle; merge trains never wait.
- **Plain owner posts:** owner posts carry what the owner needs, not the working detail. Beyond answering the owner, tell them their steps (tap, approve, test, typed OK, decide), real problems and outcomes worth knowing (landed, merged, failed, blocked, a decision or scope change); when no step is theirs, send only a short status: one point per task, or one line for a single task, each led by a bold task label, never a line saying nothing needs them. Leave out the working detail of gates, helpers and other spawned work (probes, test counts, round-by-round findings, file lists) unless asked: it stays in their full reports and the evidence store. This binds the controller's own posts to the owner, relays included. Asked with the native question method, a card holds only the short question and short options, the pick marked; the context, items and each pick's reason go above it as formatted text. Posts use the owner's time zone and bold section labels. Asks, plans and explanations lead with problem, fix and skip; "(my pick)" is the best option on the evidence, not the owner's leaning.
- **Free first:** no paid plans; reviews run on the subscription.
- **Host-local talk:** a host never mentions another host's setup unless asked.
- **Close what you spawn:** on relay, close each thread or session you started (gate, gatekeeper, lane) once it is fully done: its output is recorded, its PRs are merged or closed, its final message was read in full and every item acted on, its result was relayed and no owner test, card, question or decision is still owed. Use your host's close action ([host contract](../docs/HOST-CONTRACT.md)); with none, record it retired (gates page). Appointment authorises it (roles page). Never a scheduled task's home thread; idleness alone never counts. With no timers, re-check open ones at session start and each owner turn.
- **Polish child:** review Lows and small UAT nits are rows on one rolling Later polish child (title starts `Polish`; without governance, one polish record in the evidence store), at most 8 rows; when full it ships as one batched PR and closes, and a fresh one opens.
- **Daily stale sweep:** at most once a day, date-stamped (on the daily run where the host schedules one, never its hourly one), send the owner one list of done, obsolete and duplicate children to approve closing (reconciler skill; `toolkit-controller-check.cjs` `stale` check).
