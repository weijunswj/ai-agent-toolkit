# Source Of Truth

This repository owns reusable AI-facing material in direct canonical paths.

## Ownership

- Canonical products: complete skill entrypoints, references, templates, and assets.
- Canonical support: contracts, validators, tests, provenance, and routing metadata.
- Local law: repository-specific rules, validation, deletion policy, and safety constraints.

## Rules

- Full working instructions stay in their direct canonical files.
- Support files must not become a second owner or a lossy replacement.
- Third-party exact and adapted files require source traceability and attribution as applicable.
- Review verdicts do not grant mutation authority.
- Repository-declared derived sections are updated only through their named source and generator.
- Credentials, private files, live exports, and product-owned files stay out of reusable surfaces.
